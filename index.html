About

I am a Research Scientist at Algocyte and a PhD student in Mathematics at CINVESTAV, advised by Ernesto Lupercio. I work at the intersection of mathematics and machine learning, studying how neural models capture mathematical structure and how their behavior can be supported by rigorous guarantees.

My research spans knot invariants and representation learning, convex equilibrium networks for the Monge–Ampère equation, and the relationship between algorithmic complexity and topology. Alongside this work, I develop and validate machine learning methods for hematological analysis at Algocyte.
