About

I am a Research Scientist at Algocyte and a PhD student in Mathematics at CINVESTAV, advised by Ernesto Lupercio. I work at the intersection of mathematics and machine learning, studying how neural models capture mathematical structure and how their behavior can be supported by rigorous guarantees.

My research spans knot invariants and representation learning, convex equilibrium networks for the Monge–Ampère equation, and the relationship between algorithmic complexity and topology. Alongside this work, I develop and validate machine learning methods for hematological analysis at Algocyte.

Research

AI for Mathematics & Knot Theory

Neural representations of knot invariants, reconstruction-based diagnostics, and the mathematical structure revealed by differences between invariant spaces.

Convex Networks & Scientific Machine Learning

Convex equilibrium potentials for the Monge–Ampère equation, derivative bounds, and residual-based certification. Research with Yingdong Lu and Tomasz Nowicki of IBM Research.

Algorithmic Complexity & Topology

Connections between algorithmic information measures and topological data analysis for characterizing structure in mathematical and scientific data.

Machine Learning for Hematology

Model development, device calibration, and statistical quality control for image-based hematological analysis.
